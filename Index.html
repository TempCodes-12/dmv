Step 01

Prepare Required Documents

⚠ Important — SSN
Most international students do not have an SSN. If that's you, start by getting the No SSN Verification Letter from the SSA before anything else.
✓ If you HAVE an SSN
  • Original Social Security Card — not laminated
  • Name must match passport & immigration docs
  • If previously verified in MO, verbal may work — but bring the card
✗ If you DON'T have an SSN (most F-1s)
  • Get a No SSN Verification Letter from the SSA
  • Find your office: ssa.gov/locator
  • Call ahead — each office has different procedures
  • Bring passport, valid F-1 visa, and I-94
  • Letter must be original and recently issued
📖 Study First
Before your permit test, download the Missouri Driver Guide (PDF). Road signs come up often — don't skip them.

Step 02

Take the Permit Test

⚠ Check Before You Go
Some locations close unexpectedly due to staffing. Always call ahead or check the website: statepatrol.dps.mo.gov
How the test works
  • 25 multiple-choice questions
  • Need 20 correct to pass
  • Miss more than 5 → automatic fail
  • Up to 2 attempts per day
  • Vision test included after written exam
Bring with you
  • Passport + F-1 Visa
  • I-94
  • Proof of address
  • SSN Card or No SSN Letter

Step 03

Get Your Learner's Permit (DMV)

What to expect
  • Visit a Missouri DMV office
  • Pay about $16–$20 — cash recommended
  • Receive a paper permit usable as ID immediately
  • Physical card arrives within 2 weeks
  • May need to redo vision test
Want REAL ID? Bring extras:
  • Foreign passport + valid F-1 visa + I-94
  • SSN card or No SSN letter
  • Two different Missouri address documents
  • Name change docs if applicable
REAL ID Quick Reference
Identity + Lawful Status
  • Foreign passport
  • Valid U.S. visa (F-1)
  • I-94
Social Security
  • SSN Card or
  • No SSN Verification Letter
MO Residence (2 docs)
  • From 2 different sources
  • Utility bill, lease, bank mail, government mail
Name Change (if needed)
  • Marriage cert, divorce decree, court order
Step 04

Take the Road Test

Before you go
  • Bring your learner's permit / ID card
  • Walk-ins: arrive early in the morning
  • Only go when you're fully ready
  • You get 3 total attempts — if you fail all 3, a special driving course is required
💡 Pro Tip — Koch Road

Koch Road is known for kind, professional examiners. Many international students prefer it. Remember the exact route after your first attempt — practice it if you need to retake.

Driving Test Tips
  • Use a car you're very familiar with — first part tests basic controls
  • Know how to operate: headlights, signals, hazards, wipers, heater, defroster
  • On slopes: turn wheels correctly + use parking brake
  • When reversing or turning: physically turn your head — don't just use mirrors
  • Practice parallel parking — usually the final part
  • Always obey the speed limit — even 1 mph over can fail you

Step 05

Get Your Driver's License

What to expect
  • Go to any Missouri DMV (see Step 3 search)
  • Pay about $16–$20 — cash recommended
  • Receive a temporary paper license immediately
  • Physical card arrives within 2 weeks
  • Vision test required again before issuance
Vision Test — What to Know
  • Read letters/numbers from a chart (like an eye doctor)
  • May test peripheral vision
  • May ask you to identify road signs
  • Wear glasses or contacts if you use them
  • Fail? You'll need a form from an eye doctor first
🪪 REAL ID Driver's License
A REAL ID lets you board domestic flights using just your license. Bring the same docs as Step 3 REAL ID.
Official guide: dor.mo.gov/driver-license/issuance/real-id/interactive-guide.html
